Scoring

Every active question starts at 100% and shares the 100-point activity score equally. A distinct incorrect checked answer deducts 20% from that question’s score only. The same incorrect choice cannot deduct points twice. If you turn an item off, its linked question is removed and the remaining active questions automatically share the full 100 points.
