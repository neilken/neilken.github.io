Mechanical Systems Motion Lab

A mechanical system uses connected mechanisms to transfer energy and produce a useful output.

How to use this lab

  1. Select a system and press Play.
  2. Trace the energy from the input, through the mechanisms, to the output.
  3. Use the labels and the system trace to identify the job of each mechanism.
