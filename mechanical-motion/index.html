How to use this explorer

Choose a concept. Watch the labeled motion, then use the definition and the “notice” statement to explain what the machine does.

Analysis rule: Name the part that moves. Then identify its path: a circle, a line, repeated straight motion, or a repeated arc.

What to identify

The big engineering idea

A mechanism can transfer motion, change its direction, change its type, or trade speed and distance for force and torque. It cannot create energy.

Virtual diagrams are simplified models for class learning. Focus on the labeled input, mechanism, and output.
