Scenario and planning rules

The community plans a new neighborhood. The budget is 100 credits. The new site must have low simulated flood risk, room for at least 100 homes, and enough added water capacity to eliminate a projected 100,000-gallon-per-day shortage.

Without an improvement, projected road demand is 650 vehicles per peak hour, and road capacity is 600 vehicles per peak hour. Added road capacity affects capacity; it does not reduce traffic demand.

School capacity and other effects have not been supplied; planners would need more evidence before a real decision.

Key terms and help

Land use: how land is used. Infrastructure: physical systems that support a community. Demand: amount needed. Capacity: amount a system can provide. Constraint: a limit a solution must meet. Criterion: a desired result. Trade-off: a benefit paired with a cost or drawback.

Road comparison: new capacity = 600 + added road capacity. Compare the result with 650. Water: compare added water capacity with the original 100,000-gallon-per-day shortage.
