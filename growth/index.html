Definitions, worked examples, and original data

Demand is the amount needed; capacity is the amount a system can provide. Compare the same units and time period. If demand is higher, shortage = demand − capacity. If capacity is higher, unused capacity = capacity − demand.

Example 1: 420 + 70 = 490 students. With 480 spaces, the shortage is 10.

Example 2: 440 students + 80 = 520; 500 spaces + 30 = 530; 10 spaces remain.

Percentage change: change ÷ original amount × 100. An increase of 30 from 120 is 25%.

Original data at 120 households: Road 450 + 200 vehicles, capacity 600 per peak hour. Water 800,000 + 300,000 gallons/day, capacity 1,000,000. Housing 300 occupied + 120 incoming households, 380 homes total. School 420 + 70 students, capacity 480.

Project choices: A costs 90 credits and adds 150,000 gallons/day of water capacity; B costs 70 credits and adds 30 school spaces; C costs 60 credits and adds 25 homes. The budget is 100 credits. These are separate fictional alternatives.
